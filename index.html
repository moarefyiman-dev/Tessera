    Path of Progression

    Explore, prepare, and descend at your own pace. Your progress saves automatically, or use Save now whenever you like.

    1. Start at the surface. Fight nearby creatures with your sword (slot 1) to earn XP, materials, and sometimes healing potions. Use E to drink a potion.
    2. Grow stronger. XP raises your level, health, and damage. Click a village villager or press T nearby to talk: train for another level and damage boost, rest to restore health for 2 gold, or set a safe spawn.
    3. Explore underground. Look for a marked cave mouth on the surface; entrances appear across the endless world, about every 240 tiles. Follow its winding tunnels downward. Each entrance connects to deeper caves and guarded chambers, where enemies grow tougher.
    4. Mine what your pick allows. Equip the pickaxe (slot 2). Basic stone and copper need rank 0; iron, ice ore, and deepstone need rank 1; gold and cinderstone rank 2; star ore rank 3; chamber brick rank 4.
    5. Meet the guardian level requirements: Guardian 1 — level 5; Guardian 2 — level 10; Guardian 3 — level 15; Guardian 4 — level 20; Guardian 5 — level 25. Each later guardian requires five more levels than the last. Enter an unlocked chamber to awaken its boss. Victories grant XP and star ore, forge the next sword and pickaxe tier, boost damage, and restore your health.
    6. Make a safe return. Set your spawn at a village, then use Teleport to spawn whenever you want to return there. Teleporting does not heal you.

    Sword & Pickaxe Forging Path

    1. Starting gear · Rank 0: Bramble blade (16 base damage) and Twig pick. Mine common stone and copper; use sword and XP to prepare for the first descent.
    2. Guardian 1 · The Root Warden: Forge the Copper sabre and Copper pickaxe. Rank 1 also breaks iron, ice ore, and deepstone.
    3. Guardian 2 · Glacier Maw: Forge Frostfang and the Frost pick. Rank 2 unlocks gold and cinderstone.
    4. Guardian 3 · Ashen Colossus: Forge the Cinder cleaver and Cinder drill. Rank 3 unlocks star ore.
    5. Guardian 4 · The Hollow King: Forge the Starforged edge and Starforged pick. Rank 4 can break guardian-chamber brick.

    Each boss victory advances both tools, adds a major damage bonus, grants star ore, and restores your health. Character levels also increase health and attack damage. The Star Eater and later guardians still reward XP, star ore, and damage after tools reach their final tier.
